Section 1 of 6

Summary

  1. LinkedIn is our biggest channel: 3,385 followers, 7 own posts in the last 8 weeks, a median of 6 reactions per post, nothing posted since 31 August. The six direct competitors sit at a median of 12 reactions per post.
  2. Not one of our updates comes from a named person. At ChannelMojo the whole feed is people (median 50 reactions on 2,094 followers); at team5pm, posts by people reach 35 and 71 against 6–27 for the company voice.
  3. Our one post above 10 reactions (32) is the only one with video, a partner and tagged colleagues: the Pacvue day at the office.
  4. The LinkedIn page tells two different stories: English visitors read a D2C text, Dutch visitors an older Amazon text. Neither carries the website’s line “The D2C Performance Agency”.
  5. Instagram is split. The website links only to @10xcrew_europe, which is not visible without login; the working account @10xcrew_agency (71 followers, 75 posts) is linked from nowhere.
  6. YouTube: 23 subscribers, 28 videos, 14,996 views. The last 10 uploads are all Shorts; a birth-year joke has 2,775 views, the Pacvue expert Short 56, the Amefa workshop 11.
  7. Competitors look like thought leaders through four things: named people, an own event series, own research data, and client cases with numbers. Seven of the ten agencies compared run a newsletter; we run none.
  8. We already own the material — an expert-group chair, a jury seat, bol Gold Partner status, five named client cases with results — and hardly show it. The proposal: fix channel identity first, then let named experts and cases carry LinkedIn, and re-use that content on Instagram and YouTube.

LinkedIn — three observations

Rhythm breaks

Seven own posts in eight weeks (0.9 a week), an empty week from 7 September and fourteen days of silence on the measurement date. The internal norm is one to two posts a week.

People beat the company voice, even on our own page

Insight and document posts reach 4–6 reactions, the post with the CCO on the jury 8; the Pacvue training video with twelve tagged colleagues reached 32. The post of 17 August (“7 in 10 shoppers abandon their cart”) links to the contact page, not to a blog.

The page tells an old story — twice

Tagline “Creating online solutions for growth.” English visitors get an About text on D2C strategy; Dutch visitors get “Kickstart and grow your Amazon and online direct sales.” Growth of +2 followers in eight days is below the “do-nothing” line for pages of this size.

Instagram — three observations

Two handles, one dead link

Header, footer and /visit-our-channels/ link to @10xcrew_europe. Six requests, in two rounds, returned the same error page as a made-up account name, so whether it still exists cannot be settled from outside.

The working account is hidden

@10xcrew_agency has 71 followers, follows 156 accounts and holds 75 posts. No page on 10xcrew.com links to it.

Content still unjudged

Bio, last post date, likes and comments are only visible with a logged-in account. The internal engagement benchmark of 0.48% cannot be applied yet.

YouTube — three observations

Low rhythm, Shorts only

Two uploads in eight weeks (23 July, 31 August); the last ten uploads are all Shorts of 13–56 seconds.

Views follow humour, not expertise

“what year did you born” 2,775 views, “Social commerce” 1,932, “office gossip” 780. The Pacvue expert Short has 56 views and the Amefa onboarding workshop 11. Likes: median 4. Comments: none on any of the ten.

Presentation errors

Titles in lower case, a language error in the most-viewed title, a client name without a capital (“workshop with amefa”), duplicated hashtags, a typo in a hashtag (#10XCRE). Who watches, and from where, only YouTube Studio can tell.

Five patterns

Named people outperform the company voice

ChannelMojo’s feed is entirely reposts of its people: 7 in 8 weeks (9 visible), median 50 reactions over those nine, top 155, on 2,094 followers. At team5pm, posts by two named leaders (71 and 35) beat every company post (6–27).

Follower count does not predict activity

e-tailize has 19,929 followers and posted last on 19 June. DEPT, at 199,164 followers, gets 56 reactions on a regular post. Reach is earned per post.

Own events are the strongest thought-leadership format

Five of the ten agencies show an event series on their own site: FolloTalks, GoSpooky Invites, Fightclub Ringside, DEPT Future Club and Adwise N3YID; ChannelMojo’s Marketplace Meetups live only on LinkedIn. None of them is in Eindhoven or Brainport.

Proof beats claims

Brightways’ Maesy case (+33% revenue year on year, TACOS under 9%) reached 25 reactions against 3–5 for its other posts. GoSpooky’s Stichting Reclame Code case posts its numbers in the copy (217K+ landing-page views, CTR 6.9% vs 3.3%). team5pm, DEPT and Adwise publish own reports and whitepapers.

Instagram and YouTube follow the business model

The social-commerce agencies keep large Instagram accounts (GoSpooky 343 posts, Fightclub 456, team5pm 661); the marketplace specialists ChannelMojo and Brightways have no confirmed account on either platform. YouTube is small for everyone: team5pm, the largest, has 220 subscribers — but posts client cases with results in every description.

What this means: five opportunities

These are recommendations drawn from the measurements above, not facts. Persona letters refer to the five buyer personas in our internal brief: A Marketplace Manager, B Head of E-commerce, C EMEA E-commerce Director, D Founder/CEO of a D2C scale-up, E Brand or marketing lead for social commerce.

1 · Fix channel identity before adding volume

What
Choose one official Instagram handle and link it from the website; give LinkedIn one About text in one language, with the tagline “The D2C Performance Agency”; correct YouTube titles and hashtags; repair the “[formidable id=3]” form on /visit-our-channels/.
Audience
All personas, at the moment they check the agency before making contact.
Effect
No dead links and no outdated positioning when a prospect clicks through from the site or from YouTube.
Measure
A monthly link check (every channel link on 10xcrew.com resolves); consultation requests from social traffic in GA4 needs login.

2 · Let named experts post, and repost them from the company page

What
The CCO (chair of the ShoppingTomorrow marketplace expert group, jury member of the bol Retail Media Awards 2026) and the Head of Social Commerce post from their own profiles; the company page reposts.
Audience
A, B and C on LinkedIn.
Effect
Reactions above our company-page median of 6, following the ChannelMojo and team5pm pattern (median 50 and 53 on people’s posts).
Measure
Reactions and comments per person post against the median of 6; LinkedIn follower growth per month needs login.

3 · Turn client cases with numbers into a recurring LinkedIn document post

What
One case a month as a document post, from the five named cases on our site (Stanley 1913: +169% visibility, 18× revenue growth; Bowers & Wilkins; Signify – Light Gallery; Body & Fit; Prénatal). None of them appeared in the last ten LinkedIn updates. A short version with the expert on camera runs as an Instagram reel and a YouTube Short.
Audience
B and D.
Effect
More reactions than our insight posts (4–6), as Brightways saw with its KPI case (25 vs 3–5). Document posts are the best-performing LinkedIn format in the benchmark we use (7.00% engagement, Socialinsider).
Measure
Reactions per case post; engagement rate per format in Page Analytics and YouTube traffic sources for the Short needs login.

4 · Start a small, recurring peer event in Eindhoven

What
A quarterly session for marketplace and e-commerce leads of Brabant brands, with a partner; announce it and post a recap with named people. The Pacvue day (32 reactions, our best post) shows the format already works for us.
Audience
A and B.
Effect
A reason for people outside 10XCREW to mention and repost us; none of the ten agencies compared has an event in Eindhoven or Brainport.
Measure
Registrations; reactions on announcement and recap; number of external posts that mention 10XCREW.

5 · Publish one own dataset per quarter, and start a newsletter to carry it

What
A short benchmark that connects marketplaces and social commerce, built on the expert group’s knowledge or on own measurements, published on the blog with a LinkedIn document post. Seven of the ten competitors run a newsletter; we have 87 blog posts this year and no way to send them.
Audience
B, C and trade journalists.
Effect
A number others cite, as team5pm does with its CMO survey; a list we own instead of an algorithm we rent.
Measure
External mentions and reposts; newsletter sign-ups; referral traffic to the dataset page in GA4 needs login.
