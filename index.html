04The opportunities

Turn the findings into useful work.

These are proposals for the marketing lead to discuss and approve. Each one has a practical output and a way to evaluate it.

01 · Foundation

Make the channel identity consistent.

Align the Instagram links, prepare consistent LinkedIn descriptions, repair the channel-links form and edit the YouTube titles.

A checked route from website to channel to contact.Check every destination and record the approved wording.
02 · Expertise

Test posts from the people doing the work.

Draft posts with the CCO and the Head of Social Commerce. Use a concrete question from their work and a practical answer. Ask them to approve the content and choose the publishing account.

An approved expert-post pilot.Compare reactions and substantive comments with company posts in the same period.
03 · Proof

Build a recurring client-case format.

Turn an approved client case into a LinkedIn document. State the problem, the intervention, the result and the period. Adapt the lesson into a Reel and a YouTube video.

A reusable case template and the first approved case.Review post interactions and relevant enquiries. The published Stanley case reports 18-fold revenue growth between 2019 and 2024; attribute that claim to the case study.
04 · Community

Develop a small peer-event proposal.

Explore a session in Eindhoven for marketplace and e-commerce leads. Define the audience, the question to discuss, a possible partner and the effort required before proposing a date.

A short event brief for a decision.If approved, track registrations, attendance and relevant follow-up conversations.
05 · Continuity

Package useful knowledge for repeat readers.

Start with a focused research or insight piece and a newsletter proposal. The website API lists 87 posts published in 2026, so review that material before creating more.

A content and distribution proposal.Assess whether an opt-in audience and recurring insight format are practical. Native documents lead the 2025 engagement-by-impressions figures in the cited Socialinsider dataset; this is context for a test, not a forecast.
